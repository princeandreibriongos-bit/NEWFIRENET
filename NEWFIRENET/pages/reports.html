    Incident timeline

      Incident claim

      Release this incident?

      This returns the ticket to the queue so another ComL can claim and update it.

      You will lose update access until you claim it again.

      Critical action

      Mark this incident as Fire Out?

      This is a permanent completion step. After you save, the incident is finished and cannot be reopened from progress updates.

      Fire Out Confirms the fire is fully extinguished for this station report.

      Make sure the field situation is clear before continuing. You will not be able to unmark Fire Out on this update.

      Urgent · MCFS decision

      Raise the live fire alarm?

      This updates every responding station and may dispatch additional stations.

      Once raised, the live alarm cannot be lowered from this queue. Confirm only if the field situation requires it.
