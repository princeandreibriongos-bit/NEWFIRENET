Mission and Vision

Guiding the FireNet service commitment

The Bureau of Fire Protection’s new Mission and Vision embody a renewed commitment that strengthens our direction, sharpens our purpose, and guides every member of the organization in delivering responsive, professional, and compassionate public service.

As we continue to evolve in the face of modern challenges, these guiding principles reflect our unwavering dedication to excellence, integrity, and genuine service to the Filipino people, ensuring safer, more resilient communities for generations to come.

Mission

We protect life, property, and the environment through an integrated system of prevention, response, and investigation, strengthening public safety and community resilience.

Vision

A modern and trusted fire and emergency service building safe and resilient communities.
